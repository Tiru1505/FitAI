## Limitations

- Trained on 15 participant(s) and tested on 3 held-out participant(s). This is a small sample, so treat the numbers as preliminary evidence that the pipeline works, not as a claim about general accuracy.
- Windows overlap in time (stride 15 of 60 frames), so windows from one recording are correlated; the split is by participant, which is what keeps the test honest.
